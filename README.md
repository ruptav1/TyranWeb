New in 1.1.88:
- **History**: search without Vietnamese marks and in any word order ("site:youtube.com" too), a time range (today, yesterday, 7 or 30 days, this month, dates picked), by time or by site (each site with its number of visits; open one to search its pages), sorted by most or least visited, latest, A→Z, Z→A. While searching, the delete button deletes every item matching the words typed.
- **Private history** (Settings → Private, off by default): pages opened in private tabs and windows go into a separate encrypted file, viewable only in History → Private. An optional 4- or 6-digit PIN is asked for there; changing or removing it takes the current one.
- **Drag and drop**: a file dropped on a page that doesn't take it, or on the tab bar, opens in a tab. Finished downloads can be dragged out of Recent downloads and the Downloads page into a page, onto the tabs, or into a folder.
- **Address bar**: suggestions match without marks and in any word order, pages opened often and lately first.
- **Change location (Tor)** in place of Cloudflare WARP: pages seem to come from the country chosen; faster over several circuits, files download over the computer's own network, per-site choice.
- **Search results in**: Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo and Brave Search results for the country picked.

New in 1.1.87:
- **Comic downloader**: one zip per download. One chapter: "One Piece - Chap 1.zip"; with the next chapters: all of them in "One Piece.zip". Inside, the pictures are in a folder per chapter ("One Piece/Chap 1/001.png"). A zip of a name already there is saved as "One Piece (1).zip". CBZ and folders are still one per chapter.

New in 1.1.86:
- **Comic downloader**: each chapter is saved right into Downloads, named after the series and the chapter ("One Piece - Chap 1.zip", or a folder "One Piece - Chap 1"), with no extra folder. Every picture is saved as a PNG (001.png, 002.png…); JPEG, WebP, AVIF and GIF pages are turned into PNG as they download.

New in 1.1.85:
- **Comic downloader**: on a page that shows a comic chapter, a pink "N pictures" button appears in the address bar (or press Ctrl+Shift+M). TyranWeb finds the chapter's pictures in reading order (lazy-loaded ones and long vertical strips too, without ads or logos), reads the series and chapter names, and saves each chapter into Downloads › Comics › series as a zip, a CBZ for comic reader apps (with ComicInfo.xml) or a folder of pictures. It can go on to the next chapters by itself.
- Toolbar buttons and the window buttons sit on small pieces of frosted glass in the clear look, so they stay readable on any wallpaper.
- Menus taller than the window scroll.
